Give the fan an agent.

An award show for fans deserved someone fighting for their best seats, best plays and proper respect.

AgencyGround Zero
RoleArt Direction / Creative

The brief

The ESPYs is an award show for the fan. The creative needed to make that idea entertaining—not just say it.

The idea

With Samuel L. Jackson hosting, we gave the fan their own agent: someone making sure they get the best seats, the best plays and the respect they deserve.
