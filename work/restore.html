05 · The Salvation Army

Restore

A familiar recovery story told from an unfamiliar point of view: the children who get a parent back.

ClientThe Salvation Army
AgencyThe Richards Group
RoleArt Direction / Creative
RecognitionGold · American Advertising Awards 2017

The perspective

People understand that donations can help someone through rehabilitation. What they may not see is the life restored around that person.

The story

The film focuses on children whose parents went through rehabilitation at The Salvation Army Adult Rehabilitation Centers. By shifting the point of view, a donated item becomes part of a much larger story about families being made whole.

Gold Winner · American Advertising Awards · 2017
